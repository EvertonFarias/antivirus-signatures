Privacy Policy

MeowGuard (antivirus for Android) · Updated November 14, 2026

Summary: the app analyzes everything on your phone. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data. There's no sign-up, no ads and no tracking tools.

1. What the app analyzes (always on the device)

  • Installed apps: package name, permissions, digital signature and the hash of the installation file, to identify malware. This list never leaves the device.
  • Links, messages and QR codes you paste, share or scan in the app: analyzed locally and discarded. We don't store the content.
  • Files (apps, app packages, ZIPs, web pages, programs and documents that may hide an app) in the Download folder, WhatsApp/Telegram documents or anywhere you choose: read on the device only to calculate their hash and check them. Photos and documents are only opened to read their first bytes, to see whether they are really an app or program. Files picked through the system file picker are copied temporarily to the app's private area and deleted right after.
  • Device settings (screen lock, security update, Wi-Fi, private DNS): only read to show recommendations.

3. Permissions

  • See all installed apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): the core function of an antivirus — finding malicious apps.
  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, optional): find and check risky files (apps, packages, web pages, disguised files) in the Download folder and in storage, including new ones that arrive there. Files are only read on the device and never uploaded.
  • Notifications: warn you when a dangerous app or file shows up.
  • Internet and network state: update the signature database, check leaked passwords and see whether your Wi-Fi is secure.
  • Request app removal: open Android's screen so you can uninstall a suspicious app. Removal only happens if you confirm.
  • VPN (optional, "Safe browsing"): a local VPN that only receives the phone's DNS lookups (site names). Dangerous sites are answered with "does not exist" right on the phone; everything else is passed to your network's normal DNS. No traffic is sent to us or to third parties, nothing is logged off the device, and the rest of your traffic doesn't go through the VPN.
  • Usage access (optional, "App lock"): only to know which app is on screen, so the apps you chose can ask to unlock. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Display over other apps (optional, "App lock"): to cover a locked app the moment it opens.
  • Call screening (optional, "Call filter"): Android only shows MeowGuard incoming calls from numbers that are not in your contacts, so it can block hidden, spoofed or (in Brazil) 0303 telemarketing numbers. MeowGuard does not read your contacts or call history; the last 10 blocked numbers stay only on your phone.
  • Biometrics: unlocking uses Android's own fingerprint, face or PIN screen. MeowGuard never sees or stores this information.

4. Storage

The app only keeps on the device: your preferences, the apps you marked as trusted, the summary of the last scan and hashes it already calculated (to avoid recalculating). Everything is deleted when you uninstall the app.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect anyone's data.

6. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD and similar laws)

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no data about you in our systems to access, correct, export or delete. You can send questions through the channel below.
